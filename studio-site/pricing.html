FAQ

Common questions

How long does a project take?

Starter sites deliver in 5 days, Professional in 7 days, and Enterprise depends on scope. We'll give you an exact timeline in your quote.

Do you host the website?

We deploy to free hosting (GitHub Pages, Vercel, Netlify) so you pay nothing for hosting. If you need a custom domain, we'll help you set that up too.

What if I need changes after delivery?

Each tier includes revision rounds. Professional and Enterprise include post-launch support. Need more changes? We offer affordable maintenance plans.

Do you work with my existing brand?

Absolutely. Send us your logo, brand colors, and content — we'll build around your existing identity. No brand yet? We'll help you create one.

How do I pay?

We use Gumroad for secure payment processing. 50% upfront to start, 50% on delivery. You're protected by Gumroad's buyer guarantee.

Still have questions?

Send us a message and we'll answer everything within 24 hours.
