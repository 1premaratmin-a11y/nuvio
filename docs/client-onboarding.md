# Nuvio — Client Onboarding Guide

## The Complete Client Workflow

### Step 1: Client Inquiry
- Client fills out the contact form on your Nuvio site
- OR client buys directly via Gumroad checkout
- You receive an email notification

### Step 2: Discovery Call (Optional but Recommended)
- Reply within 24 hours
- Schedule a 15-30 min call (Google Meet, Zoom, or phone)
- Ask:
  - What does your business do?
  - Who is your target audience?
  - What websites do you like? (references)
  - What pages do you need?
  - What's your timeline?
  - Do you have branding (logo, colors, fonts)?
  - Do you have content (text, images) or need help with that?

### Step 3: Send Proposal/Quote
Use the proposal template below. Send via email within 24 hours of the call.

### Step 4: Client Pays Deposit
- Send the Gumroad checkout link for their tier
- 50% upfront (they pay on Gumroad)
- 50% on delivery (send the second Gumroad link when site is done)

### Step 5: Project Kickoff
- Send the client intake form (below)
- Request: logo, brand colors, content, images, domain preference
- Start building from the appropriate template
- Customize design, content, colors, images

### Step 6: Build & Review
- Build the site (1-5 days depending on tier)
- Deploy to a preview URL (GitHub Pages sub-repo or Vercel)
- Send preview link to client
- Collect feedback
- Make revisions (1-3 rounds depending on tier)

### Step 7: Launch
- Client pays remaining 50% via Gumroad
- Deploy to final URL (GitHub Pages or client's custom domain)
- Send handoff document with login info, repo link, and instructions
- 30 days post-launch support (Professional tier and above)
