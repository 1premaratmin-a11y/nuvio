About

We capture the silence between moments — the breath before a vow, the glance that lingers a heartbeat too long, the light that turns ordinary into timeless.

Studio Noir was founded in 2014 by Elena Vance and Marcus Cole, two photographers united by a belief that luxury is not loud. Over a decade, we've photographed 300+ weddings, editorial portraits, and commercial campaigns across four continents — always with the same obsession: composition, shadow, and the decisive moment.

Our work has appeared in Vogue, Harper's Bazaar, and Monocle. We've shot for brands like Aesop, Hermès, and The New York Times. But our favorite frames are the ones that never get published — the ones that hang in someone's hallway and stop them every morning.
