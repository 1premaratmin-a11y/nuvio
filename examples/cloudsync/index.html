Features

Everything you need to keep files in sync

A complete toolkit for storage, sharing, and collaboration — secure by default and fast everywhere.

Real-time sync

Changes propagate to every device in milliseconds. Work offline and let CloudSync reconcile the moment you reconnect.

End-to-end encryption

Files are encrypted on your device before they ever reach our servers. Zero-knowledge by design — only you hold the keys.

Team collaboration

Share folders with granular permissions, comment on any file, and see who changed what with detailed activity logs.

Version history

Restore any file to any point in the last 365 days. Never lose work to an accidental overwrite or a bad save again.

Smart backups

Schedule automatic backups of entire projects. Delta-only uploads keep backups fast and bandwidth-friendly.

Blazing speed

Edge-accelerated transfers put your files close to every team, anywhere — average 3.2× faster than legacy storage.
