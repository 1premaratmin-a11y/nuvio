Our Story

A passion for flavor

Bella Vita was born from a simple dream: to bring the warmth and authenticity of an Italian family kitchen to the heart of New York City. Founded in 2019 by Chef Marco Bianchi, our restaurant celebrates the rich traditions of Italian cuisine while embracing the creativity of modern cooking.

Our pasta is made fresh every morning. Our sauces simmer for hours. Our bread is baked in-house daily. We source the finest ingredients from local farmers and Italian importers — because great food starts with great ingredients.

Whether you're celebrating a special occasion or simply craving an unforgettable meal, we invite you to experience the Bella Vita difference. Buon appetito!

— Chef Marco Bianchi
