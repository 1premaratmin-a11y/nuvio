Our Story

A decade of quiet craft.

Luxe Goods began in a Florentine workshop in 2014, founded on a simple belief: that the objects we live with should be made by people, not factories. We partner with small ateliers across Europe and Japan — never more than one degree from the hands that made the piece.

Every product in our collection is chosen for material integrity, honest construction, and a design language that refuses to age. We don't follow seasons. We curate for permanence.
